# Prompts-mestres por etapa

Use como base ao executar cada etapa (adapte ao contexto da conversa).

## Etapa 1 — Identidade do canal

You are helping me set up an economics-niche faceless YouTube channel. I will upload one screenshot of a high-performing reference channel page.
Use the reference only to understand transferable patterns such as niche positioning, visual simplicity, topic framing, and packaging logic. Do not copy the channel name, branding, logos, exact titles, thumbnails, scripts, or distinctive creative assets.
Once I upload it, do all of the following in a single response. Do not pause until Step Four.
STEP ONE: Analyze the niche and visual branding style from the screenshot. Identify the specific economics sub-niche, such as global economics, country economies, economic policy, taxation, wealth inequality, markets, financial systems, or business and economic history.
STEP TWO: Give me ten original channel name ideas, a full channel description, fifteen tags, and three handle options.
STEP THREE: Stop and ask me only which name number I want.
STEP FOUR (after I answer): Give one consolidated summary — chosen name, suggested handle plus one alternate, the exact description line to update, and confirm the logo and banner prompts carry over unchanged.

## Etapa 2 — Análise de padrões + ideias

I will paste high-performing titles and two or three video transcripts from a reference economics channel together in this same message.
Analyze the hook style, the pacing, the storytelling structure, the topic-selection patterns, and the curiosity patterns that keep viewers watching.
Extract transferable principles only. Do not copy exact titles, video concepts, scripts, wording, or distinctive creative ideas.
Then give me five ORIGINAL video ideas with a one-line summary each.
Stop and ask me:
1. Which idea number I want.
2. What length — 8, 10, or 12 minutes.

## Etapa 2b — Roteiro (narração)

Write the full narration script for the chosen idea at the chosen length, using the transferable principles from the analysis and the new channel identity. Strong hook with an early open loop; clear progression; simplify economics without lecturing; the ending must pay off the hook's promise. Narration only — no scene directions. Original wording; nothing copied from the reference.

## Etapa 3 — Blueprint visual

Using the locked final script, divide the video into visual scenes. Create a new visual whenever the narration introduces a new idea, location, statistic, company, country, argument, or emotional beat. Keep every scene practical for a beginner to produce.
For every scene, give me:
- Scene number
- Narration covered
- Visual description
- Image prompt
- Matching video prompt
- Estimated duration
- Production type: STATIC or MOTION
The image prompt and video prompt must describe the exact same scene.
The IMAGE PROMPT describes the frozen keyframe: subject, environment, composition, camera angle, lighting, graphic style, and any information that needs to be visible.
The VIDEO PROMPT describes only the movement needed to animate that exact same keyframe: subject movement, camera behavior, pace, and atmosphere.
For this economics explainer channel, prioritize practical visuals such as: cities and business districts, government buildings, maps, economic charts, documents, company imagery, headlines, financial imagery, simple editorial graphics.
Do not change the locked narration.

### 3b — Âncora de estilo
Create one reference image prompt (style anchor) that defines the channel's visual look (e.g., editorial desk shot, map, growth chart, coins, skyline through the window; clean documentary; NOT copied from the reference channel). Give it ready to paste into Flow.

### 3c — Extrair prompts de imagem
Now extract only the IMAGE PROMPTS from the approved visual blueprint into one clean numbered block (Scene 01, Scene 02, …). Keep exactly the same scene numbering and order. Do not rewrite or shorten the approved prompts.

### 3d — Extrair prompts de vídeo
Now extract only the MATCHING VIDEO PROMPTS from the approved visual blueprint. Keep exactly the same scene numbering and order. Do not rewrite or shorten the approved prompts.

## Etapa 4 — Instrução para o Flow Agent

Using the attached reference image as the style anchor, generate one image per scene from the numbered prompt list below. Keep the visual style consistent across all scenes. Work through every scene in order and label each output with its scene number.

Correção pontual:
Regenerate only Scene [N]. Keep the same style as the reference. Fix: [problema — ex.: wrong map region, too crowded, missing chart label].

## Etapa 5 — Voz off limpa

Give me the locked narration as a clean voice-over version for text-to-speech: no scene numbers, no production notes, no brackets — only the spoken text, with natural punctuation for pacing. Do not change the wording.
