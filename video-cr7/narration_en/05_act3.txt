The day before Portugal played Ireland, in November twenty twenty-five, Cristiano told reporters he really liked the Irish fans, and promised to behave. The next day, with Portugal two-nil down at the Aviva Stadium, he looked at an Irish defender and mimed wiping away fake tears. A few minutes later, he spun around and drove an elbow into Dara O'Shea's back.
The referee showed yellow. VAR called him over. And the yellow became red: the first red card of his career in two hundred and twenty-six games for his country. On the way to the tunnel, while fifty thousand people threw the crying gesture right back at him, Cristiano stopped, looked up at the stands, clapped sarcastically, and gave two thumbs up.
At the end of season three of The Boys, Homelander loses control in front of a crowd and does the worst thing imaginable. And the crowd cheers. In Dublin, the crowd did the opposite: they filmed it, laughed, and mocked him. That's when a lot of people realized the hero's smile was starting to look a lot like the villain's sneer.
