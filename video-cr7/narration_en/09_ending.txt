At the end of The Boys, Homelander loses his powers and, in front of the cameras, humiliates himself begging for his life, while the people who once adored him switch off their TVs in disgust. The series ends with him having no control at all over how he'll be remembered.
The letter Cristiano read today goes in the opposite direction. In it, he admits that he also failed, he thanks kit men and physios who never appeared in the photos, and he writes that no captain walks alone. Which is maybe the strangest line to hear from someone who spent the last four years walking alone out of every place where he wasn't the center.
He still plays for Al Nassr, and he's still chasing the thousandth goal of his career. So the story isn't over. But the last image of him in a Portugal shirt wasn't a goal, or a trophy. It was that man sitting on the bench in Oslo. The same image the internet put next to Homelander.
Homelander never found out who he was once he stopped being the strongest man in the room. Cristiano Ronaldo, at forty-one, still has the chance the character never got.
