## ROTEIRO

**[CLIP 1 — Romário, podcast, abr/2025, em português com legenda]**
> "It's a very bad generation."

NARRATOR:
For a year and a half, the man who won Brazil the 1994 World Cup kept saying the same thing. And Brazil kept calling him bitter.

On November 14th, 2026, in Singapore, Japan beat Brazil three–two. In stoppage time. With Brazil's coach sent off.

**[CLIP 2 — imagens do gol do Sakamoto + expulsão]**

A few hours later, Romário posted eleven words.

**[TEXTO NA TELA, marcado]** *"Ancelotti, that's enough. Pack your things and get out."*

It was the second time Japan had beaten Brazil in thirteen months. Before that, in the entire history of football, it had never happened once.

So how did the most successful national team on earth become a team that loses to Japan twice?

**— 0:50 —**

June 2002. Yokohama. Ronaldo scores twice against Germany and Brazil lift their fifth World Cup. Ronaldo, Rivaldo, Ronaldinho. Three Ballon d'Or winners in one attack.

**[CLIP 3 — final de 2002]**

Nobody in that stadium knows it is the last one.

2006: out to France. 2010: out to the Netherlands. 2014, at home, in a semi-final: Germany seven, Brazil one.

**[CLIP 4 — 7×1]**

That night was supposed to be the bottom. For ten years, Brazil had one answer to every question, and his name was Neymar.

He broke his back in 2014. He lost to Belgium in 2018. In 2022 he scored in extra time against Croatia, equalled Pelé's record, and watched his team go out on penalties before he could take one.

**[CLIP 5 — Neymar chorando, 2022]**

**— 2:10 —**

In April 2025, Romário sits down on a podcast and is asked about the national team.

**[CLIP 6 — Romário, mesma entrevista, trecho maior]**

He says this generation is very bad. He says Brazil no longer has players who decide games.

The reaction is the usual one. A legend from the past who cannot accept that football changed.

Six months later, in Tokyo, Brazil lead Japan two–nil at half-time. They lose three–two. It is the first defeat to Japan in the history of the national team.

**[CLIP 7 — Japão × Brasil, out/2025]**

It is treated as an accident.

**— 3:10 —**

Brazil's answer is to hire the most decorated coach in club football. Carlo Ancelotti. Five Champions League titles.

In May 2026, before he has managed a single World Cup match, the federation extends his contract to 2030. Nine and a half million euros a year. The best-paid national team coach in the world. The federation's president calls it a historic day.

**[CLIP 8 — anúncio da renovação]**

Seven weeks later, at MetLife Stadium, Erling Haaland scores twice. Norway two, Brazil one. Round of sixteen.

**[CLIP 9 — gols do Haaland]**

It is Brazil's worst World Cup since 1990. Neymar comes off the bench, scores a penalty, and it is not enough.

**[CLIP 10 — Neymar após o jogo]**
> "It started here and it ended here. Now it's over."

The last player who carried Brazil is gone. And the drought is now twenty-four years. The longest in the country's history.

**— 4:20 —**

And then, for a moment, it looks like Romário was wrong.

Brazil beat Australia four–two. They beat India four–nil. Vinícius is scoring. Estêvão is scoring. The coach says he is happy with the young players.

**[CLIP 11 — gols contra Austrália/Índia]**

That feeling lasts six weeks.

**— 4:50 —**

Singapore. November 14th. Raphinha wears the number ten and the captain's armband.

Kubo scores. Nakamura scores. Japan two, Brazil nil.

**[CLIP 12 — os dois gols do Japão]**

Then Vinícius hits one from outside the box into the top corner. And then he scores again. Two–two.

**[CLIP 13 — os dois gols do Vini]**

Endrick hits the post. Brazil are on top. This is the part of the story where Brazil used to win.

In the ninety-first minute, João Gomes loses the ball in midfield. Brazil's players stop and appeal for a foul. Japan do not stop.

**[CLIP 14 — gol do Sakamoto, por inteiro]**

Three–two. Ancelotti is sent off. Raphinha is sent off.

After the match, the coach blames the referee.

**— 6:00 —**

But look at what actually happened. Brazil's best player scored two brilliant goals. And Brazil still lost to Japan.

That was Romário's point all along. Not that Brazil has no talent. That Brazil has nobody who decides.

**[CLIP 15 — ESPN FC]**
> "It used to be smiles and samba. Now that Brazil shirt is clearly so heavy for whoever wears it."

**[CLIP 16 — ESPN FC]**
> "There's nowhere for them to go."

Five defeats in twenty-one matches under Ancelotti. Two of them to Japan. One knockout win at a World Cup in eight years.

**[GRÁFICO — contador: 24 YEARS]**

The next World Cup is in 2030. If Brazil do not win it, the wait reaches twenty-eight years. And the man in charge has a contract that runs until the day it ends.

**— 7:10 —**

Ancelotti says his team played the way he wanted for seventy minutes. The federation has said nothing.

Romário has said one more thing. That the president needs the courage to make a decision. And that the time is now.

**[CLIP 17 — Romário, 2025, a mesma frase da abertura]**

For a year and a half, Brazil decided he was a bitter old striker who could not accept that the game had moved on.

Japan have now beaten Brazil twice.

So was Romário bitter? Or was he the only one who was still watching?

**[FIM — sem "inscreva-se"]**
