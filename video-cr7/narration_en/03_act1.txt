The first time the world saw it clearly was one night in October twenty twenty-two, at Old Trafford. Manchester United were beating Tottenham two-nil, one of their best performances that season. And Cristiano, who had returned to the club a year earlier as the prodigal son, was watching it all from the bench. In the closing minutes, when Erik ten Hag wanted to bring him on, Ronaldo refused. He stood up, and walked down the tunnel before the final whistle, leaving his teammates to celebrate without him.
Three weeks later, he was sitting across from Piers Morgan, saying he felt betrayed by the club and had no respect for the manager. Within days, his contract was terminated.
In season three of The Boys, the company that runs the heroes decides Homelander will share leadership of the team with Starlight, the hero the public has started to love more than him. He smiles for the cameras, accepts the announcement, and spends the rest of the season working to make it fail. Because for a character like this, being second in line hurts more than any defeat.
Four years later, in October twenty twenty-six, Ten Hag talked about that period again in an interview with The Athletic, and he used a line worth remembering, because it's coming back before the end of this video. He said Ronaldo let the team down, and let him down, because they had planned with him.
