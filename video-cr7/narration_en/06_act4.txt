In season four, Homelander's best-kept secret is a tiny detail: he's going grey. He plucks the white hairs in secret, and has his hair dyed. Because a hero who sells himself as eternal can't be seen getting old.
Cristiano built his entire career on his body. And in twenty twenty-six, at forty-one, that body started sending the bill. In February, a thigh injury ruled him out three months before the World Cup. In June, in the opener against DR Congo in Houston, Portugal drew one-all. He went the whole game without making an impact, heard the opposing fans chant Messi's name, and earned a brand new nickname online: Homelando. His name, fused with Homelander's.
The body still had a few flashes left. He scored twice in a five-nil rout of Uzbekistan, and against Croatia he finally scored the first knockout goal of his World Cup career. But in the round of sixteen, Spain won it with a Merino goal in stoppage time, and his last World Cup ended with Cristiano in tears on the pitch in Dallas. The nickname, which was supposed to last a week, stuck.
