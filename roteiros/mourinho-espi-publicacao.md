In July 2026, Real Madrid let Gonzalo García go to Fulham and, within hours, paid Levante's €25 million release clause for a 21-year-old striker Hull City had reportedly all but signed. To make room for him, José Mourinho left Endrick on the bench, and almost nobody understood why.
