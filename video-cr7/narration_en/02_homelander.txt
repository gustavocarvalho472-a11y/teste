For anyone who hasn't watched the show: Homelander is the strongest superhero on the planet. He flies, he shoots lasers from his eyes, he's practically indestructible, and his face is on every billboard in America. But The Boys shows what happens when the cameras switch off. And there, he's a man who needs to be adored every second of the day, who can't share the stage with anyone, and who, when somebody takes the spotlight away, reacts like a child who lost his favorite toy. Except this child can bring down an airplane.
What's terrifying about the character is realizing that no power in the world fixes the one thing he can't survive: no longer being the center. And if you look closely at Cristiano Ronaldo's last four years, you'll see that same weakness showing up again, and again, each time harder to hide.
